The Difference

Same gold glow. None of the upkeep.

Real Gold

Beautiful, but breakable

Gorgeous in theory — soft to the touch, easy to scratch, and priced well beyond everyday wear.

Gold-Plated

A finish that fades fast

A thin layer sits on top of base metal and wears through in months, leaving a dull grey underneath.

Timeless Gold-Tone

Built for the long run

A rich, durable gold-tone finish designed to resist tarnish, water, and daily wear — season after season.

*Timeless jewelry is not made of real gold and is not gold-plated. Each piece is finished in a durable gold-tone coating designed for everyday wear — the look you want, without the cost or fragility of the real thing.
